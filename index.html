Import Buyer Order Sheet

Upload an Excel, CSV, or supported buyer-order PDF. The system detects order details and size quantities, then lets you review everything before adding it.

The importer detects standard columns or separate Buyer/Style/Color rows above the size headers, with each PO immediately left of its size quantities. It also reads H&M Size/Colour Breakdown, C&A colour-size order tables, and ASOS production summaries in text-based PDFs. Extract ZIP/RAR files first and choose the relevant PDF; scanned PDFs and label artwork are not order sheets. If Country or Shipment Date is missing, enter it in the preview. CSV works offline; Excel and PDF support load parsers from the web when needed, but workbook/PDF contents stay in this browser.

Choose a buyer order sheet to start. Your file contents stay in this browser.

Cutting & Fabric Milestone Messages

A draft is created when total cutting across all POs and colors for one Buyer + Style crosses 25%, 50%, or 75% of that style's total ordered quantity. Fabric requisition and received quantities are also totaled for the Buyer + Style across colors. This browser app prepares messages but cannot send them automatically; review and send from WhatsApp or your email app.
